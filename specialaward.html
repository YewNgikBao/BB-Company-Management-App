Awarded for Members in recognition of the completion in the area of Physical Recreation, Adventurous Journey, Skills and Community Service.

Minimum age to start:
  • Bronze Level - 14 years old
  • Silver Level - 15 years old
  • Gold Level - 16 years old

President’s Award

The application must be at least 15 years and an NCO in the Company.

The maximum age to apply for this award is by 19 years old, based on calendar age.

Requirements

  • ✅ NCO Proficiency Star
  • ✅ Christian Education (Advance Level)
  • ✅ Drill (Advance Level)
  • ✅ Recruitment (Basic Level)
  • ✅ Three Year Service Badge
  • ✅ In Group A, B, C, D:
  • • 2 Basic Level badges
  • • 4 Advance Level badges

Founder’s Award

Must be a President’s Award holder.

The maximum age to apply for this award is by 20 years old, based on calendar age.

Requirements

  • ✅ Must continue membership in Senior Section at least two years
  • ✅ 40 hours of community service
  • ✅ Undertake a project as approved by the national committee
  • ✅ Attends an interview by the State Commissioner with the presence of the applicant’s Captain and Chaplain (if possible)
  • ✅ Character Assessment by:
  • • Parent / Guardian
  • • Form Teacher
  • • Captain
  • • Chaplain
