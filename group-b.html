Camping

Basic level
  • Members applying for this award must have attended a course on camping for a period of 12 lessons and have spent a total of four(4) consecutive days under canvas and have passed a test on the subjects below:
By practical, oral or written tests, show an adequate knowledge of :
  • the various types of tents, their particular uses, advantages and disadvantages.
  • ancillary equipment, such as fly sheets, groundsheets and bedding, their correct use and treatment.
  • personal health and hygiene while camping
  • the importance of good campsite selection and layout.
  • the country code.
Prepare at least a meal in a group of three or less and show the proper way of starting a fire including preparing a kitchen. Proper hygiene should be observed. With the assistance of three Members who do not hold any Camping Awards, carry out the pitchingm striking and packing of a tent completely and correctly for a short period of time as set by the instructor. If possible, the tent should not be a lightweight tent. Be able to built a gadget for camping use. Basic knots that a Member should be able to tie easily.
  • reef knot
  • figure-of-eight
  • clove hitch
  • round turn and two half hitches
  • double sheet bend
  • timber hitch
  • boutline hitch
  • bowline
  • double black wall hitch
  • marine spike hitch

■ Advanced Level

Members applying for this award must have attended a course on camping for a period of 12 lessons and have spent a total of four (4) consecutive days under canvas and who have passed a test on the subjects covered as below.

  • By practical, oral or written tests, show an adequate knowledge of:
    • the care, maintenance and storage of camping, cooking and other equipment for the purpose of the camp.
    • necessary arrangements for sanitation, hygiene and safety i.e. latrines, rubbish disposal, food storage, water supply, washing facilities, fire precautions, etc.
  • Prepare at least a day's meal in a group of three or less and show the proper way of starting a fire including preparing a kitchen. Proper hygiene should be observed.
  • With the assistance of three Members who do not hold any Camping Awards, set up a campsite which include the tent, a kitchen, a toilet, campfire and fencing around the site for a limited period of time as set by the instructor.
  • Basic knots that a Member should be able to tie easily:
    • timber hitch and half turn
    • rolling hitch
    • fishermen bend
    • running bowline
    • French bowline
    • slip knot
    • middleman and butterfly knot
    • square lashing
    • diagonal lashing
    • sheer lashing
    • gym lashing
    • round lashing
    • plus other knots at basic level

Expedition

■ Basic Level

  • The theory syllabus should cover the following parts:
    • Packing the rucksack, types of rucksack, equipment needed for the expeditions and how to pack.
    • Simple cooking. Ability to cook a simple meal under camp conditions.
    • Basic understanding on the method of use for map and compass reading.
    • Camp craft, Living in a tent.
    • Country code.
  • Practical (by foot)
    • Undertake a walk of at least 16 km in open country (preferably rural/in the jungle) following a route selected beforehand, and prepare an interesting log of the journey. An adult leader/officer must accompany the trainee/s throughout the journey.
    • Spend one night under canvas using lightweight equipment, including the cooking of an evening meal and breakfast.

■ Advanced Level (Training)

  • Safety Precautions. Safety factors inherent in sound expedition techniques, discipline on the move. Procedure for dealing with an accident in the type of country used for expedition.
  • Map and compass. The nature of map, scale, conventional signs, contours, map reference, route planning, setting of a map, elementary compass work.
  • Camp Craft. Care of tents, including pitching and striking, care and cleaning of campsite, arrangements for cooking, sanitation refuse disposal and fire precautions.
  • Cooking. Planning and preparation of hot, sustaining meals under conditions to be expected on the test.
  • Survival. Basic survival skills.
  • Practical Undertake an expedition on foot, of at least 24km across normal country, such as agricultural or common land, within a cycle or bus ride forms most city districts and housing estates. The expedition is to be completed in two days, including one night spent in tents or bivouacs. Roads are to be used as little as possible. At least two hot meals are to be prepared during the expedition; no food may be bought enroute, but drinking water may be drawn from houses. All the necessary equipment will be carried. A similar level of difficulty can be done for expedition in water.

Fire and Rescue

■ Basic Level

  • Theory
    • Basic knowledge of Fire Science
    • Methods of extinguishing fire
    • Common causes of fire
    • Classification of fire
    • Escape from fire and smoke
  • Practical
    • Fireman's lift.
    • Emergency Methods of Removing Casualties.
    • Visit to the Fire Station or Civil Defence Department.

■ Advanced Level

  • Theory
    • Fire Service Organisation / Civil Defence Organisation
      • Organisation and structure.
      • Functions and duties.
      • Rank structure and responsibilities.
    • Fire Fighting and Rescue Equipment
      • Types.
      • Construction.
      • Maintenance.
      • Method Operations.
    • Fire Service Appliances
      • General Structure.
      • Breaking Tools.
      • Rescue Equipment.
    • Water Supply
      • Fire Hydrant.
      • Pools and others.
  • Practical
    • Method of operating a fire hydrant including connecting the hose, turning on the system, cleaning as well as storing the hose and equipment.
    • Method of escape from a burning building including ascending and descending methods.
    • Visit a fire and rescue station or the local civil defence centre.
