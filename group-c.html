Citizenship

Requirements

■ Basic Level

Members should attend a course of at least covering a period of 3 months on one of the following topics:

  • The National Government
  • Judicial systems
  • Anti-drug abuse
  • Crime prevention

■ Advanced Level

Either individually or in a group, make a further detailed study on the above topics. The study must cover the following areas:

  • The respective agencies involved in the topic chosen
  • Their contacts and various people involved in running those agencies
  • Other NGO's involved in assisting these agencies
  • Make a few visits to the agencies involved
  • How to get yourself involved in supporting the government's efforts

Community Service

Requirements

■ Basic Level

  • Demonstrate a working knowledge and understanding of the role and work of the various community service organisations within the community e.g. Special Service for the Handicaps, children and elderly, members' Home and Training Centre etc.
  • Volunteer at a particular service organisation for at least 15 hours.
  • Prepare a detailed report on the project and evaluate the work done, its effect on the recipient and the understanding the members have gained. The report can be in the format of written report or presentation using visual aids.

■ Advanced Level

  • Members must have attended a course covering the following topics: The problems of those involved in any of the following areas of community service: Children, Youth, Adults, Elderly, the Handicapped, Drug Addicts, Immigrants, Refugees.
  • Conduct a detailed study of the work of service organisations operating in Malaysia aside from those done at Basic Level. Members must have made visits to a few of the organisations listed in their study.
  • Plan and carry out a service project of at least 25 hours duration with any organisations.

Environmental Conservation

Requirements

■ Basic Level

  • Know the minimum impact code.
  • Suggest ways of re-using the following items:
    • glass jars
    • plastics ice-cream containers
    • books, Christmas cards, greeting cards
    • postage stamps
    • toys
    • old clothes
  • Investigate and report on local opportunities for recycling three of the following items:
    • newspaper
    • glass
    • scrap metal
  • Prepare a scrapbook of twenty newspaper and magazine articles about conservation and write a thoughtful comment about each article.

■ Advanced Level

  • Collect plastic, metal and glass rubbish from beach, lakeshores or stream and explain how three of these items could harm wildlife.
  • Present a 5-minutes talk to a small group of people.
  • Describe six things Malaysian motorists can do to conserve fossil fuels.
  • Prepare an eye-catching and informative feature page (pages) for a teenage newspaper or magazine about rare and endangered plants and animals in Malaya.
  • Interview a member of a non-government conservation group about the group's activities. Take part in one of their projects to improve a local place e.g.
    • remove rubbish and weeds
    • plant trees and shrubs
    • plant and maintain a flower garden

Safety

Requirements

■ Basic Level

Members are to complete the following tasks:

  • Present on:
    • The causes of accidents and prevention of accidents from happening at home, school, water, road and sports.
    • Personal physical safety.
  • Organize a safety campaign either at school or at a local church on the prevention of accidents and personal physical safety.
  • Conduct a risk assessment of your Company Parade meeting place.

■ Advanced Level

Research on Cyber Safety for young people and present on:

  • Digital Citizenship
  • Privacy and Security
  • Principles of Cyber Security
  • Cyberbullying
  • Personal Data and Identity Theft

Any other topics deems appropriate by the Officer/Instructor.

Social Entrepreneurship

Requirements

■ Basic Level

Individual member participates in an entrepreneurship project with a social enterprise. Upon completion of the project, the Member(s) will need to present on the summary of the project highlighting:

  • Goals and objectives
  • Venue & Time
  • Problem statement and solutions
  • Stakeholders
  • Impact on the local community
  • Financial
  • Reflection on the project

Alternatively, a research study can done on a project done by a social enterprise outside of Malaysia and presents on the summary with similar points as above.

■ Advanced Level

Members individually or in a group of up to five individuals to propose, plan and execute a 3 months entrepreneurship project that positively impacts a local community.

The business project idea has to be approved by the Company Officer before commencement. Upon completion of the project, the Member(s) will need to present the outcome of the project highlighting:

  • Goals and objectives
  • Venue & Time
  • Problem statement and solutions
  • Stakeholders
  • Impact on the local community
  • Financial
  • Reflection on the project

Sustainability

Requirements

■ Basic Level

Member individually or Members in a group of up to ten individuals within a Company undertakes a project contributing to a Sustainable Development Goal (SDG). The project has to:

  • Contribute to at least one (1) SDG.
  • Directly impact positively to a local community within the region/district of the BB Company.

To be awarded this badge, the member(s) must:

  • Complete the project over a minimum of 3 sessions with a total of minimum 15 hours.
  • Submit a post-project report in either a written report, presentation slides or a video presentation.

■ Advanced Level

In a group of a minimum of two members and up to twenty members from at least two different Companies, undertake a project contributing to a Sustainable Development Goal (SDG). The project has to:

  • Contribute to at least one (1) SDG that has not been undertaken at the Basic Level by the individual member.
  • Directly impact positively to a local community within the region/district of the BB Company.

To be awarded this badge, the member(s) must:

  • Complete the project over a minimum of 6 sessions with a total of minimum 30 hours.
  • Submit a post-project report in either a written report, presentation slides or a video presentation.
