Awarded to members who have attended a course and training in gymnastics by a qualified and competent instructor. The course and training must cover a period of three months. Members have to complete any two of the above categories.


■ Advanced Level

Awarded to members who have attained basic level during the previous session or who have attended further training in gymnastics. Members must have completed a further four of the above categories not completed in Basic Level.

Physical Training

Requirements

■ Basic Level

Theory

Awarded to members who have attended a course on the following:

  • The basic principles of physical training.
  • Understanding the need for warm-ups.

Practical

  • Attended training sessions comprising of 1/2 hour per training. Training sessions should include warming up, circuit training, farley, short distance jogging, variable training, movement training, physical efficiency exercise and warm down.
  • Show satisfactory improvement during training.
  • Achieve a standard of performance appropriate to age and natural ability.

■ Advanced Level

Theory

Awarded to members who have attended a course on the following:

  • Knowledge on the functions of muscles, blood circulation, brain system during and after training.
  • Principles of anaerobic training, aerobic training, and lactate threshold.

Practical

  • Have shown further improvement in his/her performance for a further period of another one (1) session.
  • Be able to lead other members in physical training. He/She should be able to show ability to lead a systematic training for the other members for preparation for sports.
  • Achieve a standard of performance appropriate to age and natural ability.

Sports

Requirements

■ Basic Level

Awarded to Members who qualify as follows:

  • Participate in the activity for a period covering at least one (1) academic year.
  • Attend training and instructional periods, and show progress in their personal performance.
  • Be actively involved in competitions in Company, School, State, National or International levels. Squad, class, sports house and club levels are not recognised.
  • Show knowledge of the sports in its wider aspect, e.g. history, rules, competitions, etc.

■ Advanced Level

Awarded to members who have attained the basic level in the previous year and who have achieved the following:

Either

  • Continue to participate actively in the same sports for another session.
  • Show further progress in the sports and have attained further skills.
  • Participate actively in competitions on a higher level than those in the basic level in which he/she should be playing against better teams such as district teams, state teams and/or national teams.
  • If it is a team sport, he/she should be playing in a regular position i.e. striker for football.

Or

  • Participate in another sport for another session and have qualified for that sport as required in basic level.

Below is the syllabus for the CANOEING AWARD, which may be used as a guideline for the other activities.

■ Basic Level

Theory

  • Types of canoe and the history of canoeing
  • Know the Water Safety Code
  • Proper way of maintaining a canoe and proper way for storing

Practical

  • Carrying a canoe single-handed or double.
  • Demonstrate on a canoe the methods of rescue i.e. T rescue and H rescue.
  • Demonstrate forward and backward paddling and emergency stop travelling in both directions.
  • Canoe for at least 1km and back.

Water Adventure

Requirements

■ Advanced Level

Theory

  • Demonstratestrate all the strokes that have been taught in the class.This includes forward and backward paddling,emergency stops,sweep strokes,recovery by means of a siap support,sculling draw,draw strokes and ferry glide.
  • Be able to carry out running repairs on the type of canoe in which the test is to be taken.
  • Answer correctly oral questions on the principles of canoe camping, on safety on the rules and on access problems in candidate's own locality.
N.B.

Members who have sat for an examination set by the Malaysian Canoe Association or other certified Canoeing Association in or outside the country and have been awarded at least a 3 Star can qualify for this award. Members who have gone for a 10-day training with 'Tunas Samudera' under the auspicious program of Kementerian Belia & Sukan Malaysia(Ministry of Youth & Sports Malaysia) would automatically be awarded the Advanced Level.
