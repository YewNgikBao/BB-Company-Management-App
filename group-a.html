Bandsman
Requirements
■ Basic Level
  • Has been an active member of a BB band for at least a year playing the instrument to be tested on.
• Theory
  • Understand the basic theory of music and gone through a written examination.
• Practical
  • Must have performed at least three (3) times to the public/non-BB audience.
  • Show knowledge in the care and maintenance of the particular instrument.
  • Read at sight any simple piece given by the examiner.
■ Advanced Level
  • Has been an active member of a BB band for a further one year playing the same instrument in which one was tested at the Basic Level.
• Practical
  • Play two pieces of music of one’s own selection.
  • Must have performed at least another three (3) times in public in the period of time.
  • Read at sight a more difficult piece given by the examiner.

Life Saving
Requirements
■ Basic Level

Awarded to Members who have passed the following examinations which conforms to the R.L.S.S. (Royal Life Saving Society / Persatuan Penyelamat Kelemasan Di Raja) Intermediate Certificate.

  • Water Safety & Rescue
    - Answer 6 questions on Water Safety & the Principles & Methods of Rescue.
  • Resuscitation Test
    - Successfully complete the test for ‘Resuscitation Award’ from RLSS.
    - Alternatively, Members with the First Aid Award (Basic) may be exempted from this requirement.
  • Water Tests

a) This test represents a situation where your friend who is a nonswimmer has slipped and fallen into the water. This casualty will be in deep water, 1m from the side. Demonstrate a reaching rescue and instruct him how to climb out. (Aids: stick, branch, clothing, towel, etc.)

b) The current has taken your friend a little further from the side and he is in deep water, 10m away from you. Demonstrate a throwing rescue using an unweighted rope and instruct him how to climb out. You will be allowed up to three (3) attempts to reach the casualty.

c) An aid that will float is available and you are a swimmer. A weak swimmer is in difficulty in deep water 20m away. Enter deep water with a straddle jump. Demonstrate how a buoyant aid can be used to rescue the casualty by an accompanied rescue. Accompany swimmer 20m back to your entry point and assist him to land.

d) You do not know the depth and conditions of the water. Take a pole, test the depth, enter the water and wade near enough to the casualty to reach him using the pole. Return the casualty to your point of entry. For test purpose, the casualty will be 4m away from the bank (at the shallow end of the pool).

e) Demonstrate a throwing rescue using an unweighted rope over 10m. Three attempts are allowed to place the rope within reach of a stationary subject. The subject should be carefully pulled to the side.

f) This test represents a situation where a casualty has sunk to the bottom and you are helping in the search. Enter the water, swim 10m and demonstrate a feet-first surface dive to the bottom, which shall be 1.5m deep. Swim for two (2) strokes underwater with the body and limbs fully submerged before surfacing and return to your point of entry using the life saving backstroke.

g) To demonstrate the action that might have to be taken in a surprise situation, the candidate will demonstrate two (2) defense actions as specified by the Examiner, e.g. Reverse, Single Leg Block, Counter to Leg Clutch & Duck Away.

h) Swim continuously for 150m. (Time Limit: 4 minutes) - 50m headup to front, 50m side stroke and 50m life saving backstroke (without arms). Members who have passed the Swimming Award (Basic) may be exempted from this requirement.

Life Saving
Requirements
■ Advanced Level

Awarded to Members who have passed all the following examinations which conforms to the R.L.S.S. (Royal Life Saving Society) Bronze Medallion. Members undertaking this level of the award must hold the First Aid Award (Basic) and Swimming Award (Advanced).

The examination will consist of three sections:

  • Water Safety & Rescue
    - Answer 6 questions on Water Safety & the Principles & Methods of Rescue (RLSS).
  • Emergency Aid
    - Members with the First Aid Award (Basic) will be exempted from the resuscitation, CPR and First Aid requirements of this award.
  • Water Tests

a) Reaching Rescues:

This test represents a situation where a non-swimmer has slipped and fallen into the water. The casualty will be in deep water, 2m from the side. Use an article of clothing, demonstrate a reaching rescue and instruct him how to climb out.

b) Throwing Rescues:

A non-swimmer is in difficulty 10m away in deep water. Demonstrate a throwing rescue using an unweighted rope and secure the casualty in a position of safety. A time limit of one (1) minute shall apply, commencing with an uncoiled and untangled rope lying on the ground at the feet of the rescuer and ending when the end of the rope has reached the casualty.

c) Cross Chest Tow:

Fully clothed, enter shallow water and swim 20m to the subject. Demonstrate the reverse and calm, reassure and approach the subject from behind. Tow 20m by Cross Chest Tow. Assist the subject to land.

d) Towing with an Aid:

A conscious non-swimmer is in difficulty in deep water 50m away. A non-rigid, non-buoyant aid is available. Taking the aid, enter deep water, swim 50m and tow the casualty 50m to end in deep water. Assist the casualty to land. Time limit - 3 minutes (male), 3 minutes 15 seconds (female).

e) Combined Rescue:

Enter shallow water and swim 20m. Submerge and search underwater for 5m to recover an object (4kg) from a depth of at least 1.8m. During the search, demonstrate the feet-first and headfirst surface dives. Exchange the object for a subject considered unconscious and not breathing; tow to the nearest point of support in deep water, and demonstrate mouth-to-mouth resuscitation for 3 cycles. Tow the casualty to shallow water and continue E.A.R for 5m. Land the casualty and place him in the recovery position.

f) A non-swimmer is in difficulty in deep water, 50m from the point of entry. No aids are available. Enter the water with a straddle jump and swim 50m to the casualty. Apply a defensive action and rescue over 50m by means of the chin tow which include a demonstration of the methods used to restrain a struggling casualty (a double restraint). On completion of the tow, support the casualty and on the signal from the Examiner, assist him to land from the deep end.

Nature Awareness
Requirements

■ Basic Level

Awarded to Members who have attended a course of instruction for a period of 3 months or 12 lessons/classes on the following subject:

  • Read and understand the ordinance survey map at a suitable scale and be able to use a compass for simple direction finding.
  • Know the country code.
  • Undertake several visits to a local habitat and note the flora and fauna found there.
  • Draw a sketch map of the habitat chosen and keep notes with the drawings of observation made during the visits.
  • Make a small collection, correctly labelled and arranged, of anyone of the following: wild flowers, leaves of trees, insects, rock fossils, shells.

Or

  • Show an interest and progress in bird watching or fishing.

Nature Awareness
Requirements

■ Advanced Level

  • Maintain visits to a local habitat, and add further specimens to the collection.
  • Undertake a walk of at least 12km and show ability to observe the natural history on route.
  • Have knowledge of the use of textbooks to identify specimens.
  • Show ability in identifying from specimens or photographs a number of any of the following: Wild flowers, trees, fungi, insects, butterflies, mammals, birds, fish, rocks, fossils, shells.
  • Understand the different soils and bedrock to be found in the various habitats and understand how a habitat builds up from bare soils.
  • Understand how habitats differ in their flora and fauna and why, and what factors affect the environment.
  • Undertake a simple project; e.g. bark rubbings, animal footprints, nature scrapbook, animal or plant sketching, etc.
